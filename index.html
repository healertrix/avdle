          How to play

          1. Read the prompt, e.g. "Pin a country the Danube flows through."
          2. Tap the map to place your pin. Tap again to move it. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.
          3. Press Drop pin. One answer per prompt.
          4. After five prompts, review every answer on the map and learn why each country counts.

          Every right answer scores, but the less charted your answer, the more it's worth:

            Who lived here? Two pins show where a famous person was born (★) and died (✝), with the dates. Type their name; the surname is enough, and small typos are fine. A hint halves the points; less famous people score more.

            Territories count for the country they belong to (Greenland is Denmark, Guam is the United States). Tiny countries have a dot so you can tap them.

            Use ☰ for the menu and Exit to leave any screen. Keyboard: arrows pan, + / − zoom, Enter drops the pin or moves on, Esc closes panels.

            Install Avdle

            Tap the Share button (□↑) in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. Avdle then opens full screen like any other app.

            Settings

            Turn off names and borders for a harder game. With the clock on, your pin drops itself when time runs out. Rooms set their own clock.
